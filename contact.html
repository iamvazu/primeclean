Questions

Answers before you call.

Is Prime Clean bonded, insured, and certified?+
Yes. Prime Clean carries general liability insurance and bonding, and holds active Women-Owned Small Business (WOSB) and California Small Business (SB) certifications. Documentation is available on request via our capability statement.
What happens during the free walkthrough?+
Jackie visits your facility in person — usually about 15 minutes — and evaluates it against our Cleanliness & Compliance checklist: restroom sanitation and supply levels, high-touch surface disinfection frequency, floor care, and OSHA-relevant items. You get the results in writing, whether or not you move forward with a quote. There's no obligation, and it's a useful second opinion even if you're happy with your current vendor.
How much does commercial office cleaning cost in the Bay Area?+
Pricing depends on square footage, frequency, and facility type. Most East Bay office accounts run on a per-square-foot monthly rate — request a quote above and we'll return a written estimate within one business day.
What NAICS codes does Prime Clean operate under?+
Primarily 561720 (Janitorial Services) and 561210 (Facilities Support Services), with 561740 (Carpet & Upholstery Cleaning) for specialty floor work.
Do you service government and public-sector facilities?+
Yes — Prime Clean is an active SAM.gov registrant and a Cal eProcure–registered small business, currently serving municipal and county facilities across the East Bay. See our Government Contracting page for certifications and NAICS detail.
