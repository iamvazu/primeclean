Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Medical & Healthcare Clinic Cleaning

What certifications does Prime Clean hold for medical and clinical cleaning?

Prime Clean's operations leadership holds active Cal/OSHA Bloodborne Pathogens Certification (Title 8 CCR § 5193, Cert. #000043147277). In addition, we are a certified Women-Owned Small Business (WOSB), State of California Small Business (#2048444), and DIR Registered Janitorial Contractor (#1001318678).

What disinfection chemistry do you use for healthcare and dental facilities?

We utilize EPA-registered List N hospital-grade disinfectants with verified kill claims against bloodborne pathogens (HIV-1, Hepatitis B & C), human coronavirus, influenza, MRSA, norovirus, and resistant bacteria, strictly honoring mandated contact dwell times.

How do you prevent cross-contamination between clinical rooms and restrooms?

We enforce an institutional 4-zone color-coded microfiber towel and flat-mop system (red for clinical restrooms, yellow for lab/prep zones, blue for waiting rooms, green for administrative areas). Mop pads and cloths are isolated and never cross-contaminate zones.

What healthcare and clinical sectors do you service in the East Bay?

We service private medical practices, dental and orthodontic clinics, biotech/life sciences research labs, urgent care centers, outpatient surgery centers, dialysis clinics, physical therapy offices, school district health offices, and aesthetic medspas across Alameda, Contra Costa, and San Francisco counties.

Can you accommodate discreet evening cleaning after clinic operating hours?

Absolutely. We schedule clinical cleaning exclusively after patient care hours or over weekends to ensure zero interference with patient appointments, maintain complete HIPAA confidentiality, and adhere to secure lock-up protocols.
