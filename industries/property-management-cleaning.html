Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Property Management & Multifamily Cleaning

What areas are included in your standard multifamily cleaning package?

Standard scopes include main lobbies, entrance glass, elevator cabs and tracks, mail/package rooms, residential corridors, stairwells, fitness centers, community clubhouses, and shared restrooms.

Can you provide services for multiple properties in our management portfolio?

Yes. We manage multi-site portfolios across Richmond, Oakland, Berkeley, San Francisco, and surrounding cities with customized scopes, dedicated supervisors, and unified invoicing.

How do you handle emergency cleanup requests (such as leaks or common area spills)?

Property management clients receive direct phone access to their assigned account supervisor for rapid-response dispatch to handle unexpected spills, trash overflows, or inspection prep.
