Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Industrial & Warehouse Floor Care

What types of machinery do you deploy for warehouse floor cleaning?

We utilize industrial electric ride-on scrubbers, cylindrical sweepers, high-CFM HEPA vacuum systems, and commercial walk-behind scrubbers suitable for spaces from 5,000 to 200,000+ sq ft.

Can you clean while our warehouse or distribution center is actively operating?

Yes. We coordinate zone-by-zone cleaning with your operations supervisor, utilizing safety cones, caution tape, and quick-drying squeegee scrubbers to prevent forklift delays or slip hazards.

Do you clean high-bay pipes, beams, and lighting trusses?

Yes. We provide high-dusting services utilizing specialized extension poles and lift equipment to remove accumulated dust, helping you maintain fire code and air quality standards.

Are your technicians insured for industrial and manufacturing environments?

Prime Clean carries $2,000,000+ in general liability coverage, comprehensive workers' compensation, and surety bonding specifically structured for industrial commercial accounts.
