Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Bank & Financial Institution Cleaning

What security vetting process do your bank cleaning technicians undergo?

Every janitorial team member undergoes thorough criminal background checks, identity verification, and drug testing. We are fully bonded and insured with strict chain-of-custody key management.

How do you handle security codes, keycards, and alarm procedures?

Our technicians strictly follow individual access code protocols, verified check-in/check-out timestamps, and perimeter deadbolt verification. Alarm setting is confirmed electronically on our supervisor dashboard.

What floor care is provided for luxury marble or terrazzo bank lobbies?

We deploy specialized high-speed stone burnishers with diamond-impregnated pads and neutral pH cleaners that enhance the natural stone luster without chemical buildup or slip hazards.
