Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Retail & Showroom Cleaning

Can retail cleaning be scheduled before our store opens in the morning?

Yes. Most of our retail clients prefer early-morning service (e.g., 6:00 AM – 9:00 AM) so the sales floor is pristine, completely dry, and freshly scented when store associates and shoppers arrive.

What types of retail flooring can you maintain?

We maintain luxury vinyl tile (LVT), polished concrete, hardwood, terrazzo, VCT, ceramic tile, and commercial carpet using manufacturer-recommended pH-neutral chemistry.

Do you handle outdoor entrance pressure washing or sidewalk cleanup?

Yes. We offer sidewalk power-washing, gum removal, and entryway canopy cleaning as part of comprehensive retail exterior maintenance packages.
