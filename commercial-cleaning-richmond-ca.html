Where is Prime Clean located in Richmond, CA?+
Prime Clean is headquartered at 353 S 24th St, Richmond, CA 94804. We provide local commercial cleaning and janitorial services throughout Richmond (Point Richmond, Marina Bay, Hilltop, Downtown, Iron Triangle, 23rd St) and surrounding Contra Costa and Alameda county communities.
How much does commercial cleaning cost in Richmond, CA?+
Commercial cleaning pricing in Richmond depends on your building square footage, cleaning frequency (daily vs. 3x/week), floor surfaces (carpet vs. hard floor), restroom count, and special requirements (medical disinfection vs. warehouse floor scrubbing). We offer free on-site walkthroughs and send transparent, itemized quotes within 24 hours.
Do you provide after-hours or weekend office cleaning?+
Yes. We specialize in seamless after-hours keycard service, evening shifts, early morning cleaning, and weekend deep resets to ensure zero disruption to your employees and operations. Day porter services are also available for daytime touchpoint maintenance.
What certifications does Prime Clean hold for government solicitations?+
Prime Clean is a certified Women-Owned Small Business (WOSB), a California Small Business Certified contractor (Cal eProcure #2041245), DIR registered (#1001318678), and active on SAM.gov (NAICS 561720). We provide prevailing-wage certified payroll and electronic audit logs.
Are your cleaners direct employees or subcontractors?+
All Prime Clean cleaning technicians are direct W-2 employees — never random unvetted subcontractors. Each crew member undergoes background checks, drug screening, and continuous training in chemical safety and security compliance.
