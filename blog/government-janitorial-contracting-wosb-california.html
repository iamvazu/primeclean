Navigating Government Janitorial Contracting: WOSB, SAM.gov & Public Sector Compliance

📍 Geographic Scope: California, Bay Area & Federal

Government procurement officers and public facility directors face strict mandates for small business participation goals, prevailing wage compliance, and certified security verifications. Here is a guide on how public sector janitorial contracts work in California.

Primary NAICS & PSC Codes for Custodial Procurement

Code Type Description

561210 NAICS Facilities Support Services
561740 NAICS Carpet and Upholstery Cleaning Services
S201 PSC Housekeeping — Custodial Janitorial Operations

Why WOSB and CA Small Business Certifications Matter

Both California state agencies (under the 25% Small Business Participation Goal) and federal agencies (under SBA WOSB set-aside programs) have statutory targets to award contracts to certified small, women-owned businesses:

  • Sole-Source and Set-Aside Eligibility: Contracting officers can direct or restrict solicitations under simplified acquisition thresholds to certified WOSB/SB vendors.
  • California DIR Prevailing Wage Compliance: All public agency cleaning contracts in California require certified payroll records submitted via the DIR eCPR system.
  • Active SAM.gov Registration: Ensures real-time verification of CAGE codes, Unique Entity Identifiers (UEI), and active federal standing.
