Commercial Post-Construction Cleaning: The 3-Phase Handover Checklist for Bay Area General Contractors

✍️ By: Prime Clean Post-Construction Crew
📅 Published: August 2026
⏱️ Read Time: 6 min read
📍 Market: Richmond, Oakland, San Francisco & East Bay

When tenant improvement (TI) or new commercial construction projects reach completion, handover delays can trigger expensive penalties. A structured, three-phase construction cleaning scope guarantees that buildings pass final owner inspection and receive Certificate of Occupancy on schedule.

The 3 Essential Phases of Post-Construction Cleaning

Phase 1: Rough Clean (Post-Framing, Drywall & MEP Rough-In)

Conducted once major electrical, mechanical, and drywall installations wrap up. Focuses on removing large debris, sweeping drywall grit, vacuuming baseboards, and prepping bare subfloors for flooring installation.

Phase 2: Light Clean / Detail Prep (Pre-Punch List)

Executed after fixture, door, and tile installation. Includes comprehensive wiping of cabinetry inside and out, scrubbing tile grout haze, HEPA vacuuming HVAC registers, and cleaning interior glass.

Phase 3: Final Turnover & Polish (Move-In Ready)

Completed 24–48 hours prior to client walk-through. Microfiber dust wipe of all walls, spotless streak-free window cleaning, scraping paint overspray and silicone from glass/hardware, deep carpet extraction, and floor waxing or burnishing.

🏗️ General Contractor Post-Construction Checklist

  • Paint overspray and adhesive sticker removal from all window glass and frames
  • HEPA vacuuming inside electrical closets, server rooms, and supply air diffusers
  • Deep sanitizing of all new restroom plumbing fixtures and partitions
  • Final floor sealing, scrubbing, or VCT waxing ready for immediate tenant occupancy
