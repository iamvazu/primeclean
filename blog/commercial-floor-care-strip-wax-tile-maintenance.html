Commercial Floor Care Guide: Stripping, Waxing & High-Traffic Maintenance

✍️ By: Prime Clean Floor Care Specialists
📅 Published: August 2026
⏱️ Read Time: 5 min read
📍 Service Area: Richmond, Oakland, Berkeley & SF

Flooring is typically the single largest capital investment inside a commercial building. Without a systematic maintenance program, Vinyl Composition Tile (VCT), terrazzo, polished concrete, and linoleum will yellow, scratch, and degrade years ahead of their expected lifespan.

The 4 Tiers of Commercial Hard-Floor Maintenance

1. Daily & Routine Maintenance (Dust Mopping & Damp Mopping)

Removing loose grit and abrasive dirt daily prevents micro-scratches in the protective wax finish. Always use neutral-pH floor cleaners to avoid dulling the finish.

2. Periodic High-Speed Burnishing (Monthly / Quarterly)

Using a 1500–2000 RPM high-speed propane or electric burnisher reheats and re-levels the floor finish, removing shallow heel marks and restoring high-gloss reflective clarity ("wet look").

3. Top Scrub & Recoat (Every 6 Months)

A deep machine scrub removes the top 1–2 soiled layers of finish without stripping down to bare tile. 2 fresh coats of high-solids commercial polymer finish are applied to rebuild the protective wear layer.

4. Full Floor Strip & Wax (Annually / As Needed)

Complete chemical stripping of all old, discolored wax down to the original tile. Neutralization, rinsing, and applying 4–5 coats of commercial-grade floor finish for complete rejuvenation.

✨ Floor Care ROI for Property Managers

  • Extends VCT and vinyl tile lifespan by up to 10+ years
  • Reduces slip-and-fall liability through high-traction, certified non-slip floor finishes
  • Creates an immediate positive visual impression for visiting clients and tenants
