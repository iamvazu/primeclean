Commercial Night Janitor / Office Cleaner
$20.00 – $24.00 / hour

Perform scheduled evening cleaning for corporate offices, tech suites, and public facilities across Contra Costa and Alameda County.

  • Empty trash/recycling, dust desks and surfaces, and disinfect high-touch areas.
  • Sanitize restrooms, restock supplies, and polish chrome fixtures.
  • Vacuum carpets and mop hard surface floors using color-coded microfiber.
  • Complete digital service sign-off at the end of each shift.
Experience: 6+ months preferred

Commercial Day Porter
$22.00 – $26.00 / hour

Maintain high-visibility corporate lobbies, multi-tenant properties, and healthcare centers during active business hours.

  • Continuous upkeep of building lobbies, elevators, breakrooms, and restrooms.
  • Respond promptly to tenant spills, supply replenishment, and light maintenance.
  • Maintain exterior entryway glass, trash receptacles, and outdoor courtyards.
  • Professional communication with building tenants and facility managers.
Experience: 1+ year customer-facing
Full-Time Specialty Route Bay Area Regional
Floor Care & Carpet Technician
$24.00 – $28.50 / hour

Lead specialized hard-floor restorative maintenance (VCT stripping, waxing, burnishing, terrazzo polishing) and commercial hot-water carpet extraction.

  • Operate low-speed floor buffers, auto-scrubbers, and high-speed burnishers.
  • Perform multi-coat commercial wax application and ceramic tile acid washing.
  • Operate truck-mount and portable hot-water carpet extraction machinery.
  • Maintain and inspect commercial machinery and chemical inventory.
