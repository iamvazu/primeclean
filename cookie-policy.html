1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website you visit. They are widely used to make websites work, work more efficiently, and to provide reporting information. Similar technologies include pixels, tags, local storage, and SDKs; in this policy, we refer to all of these collectively as "cookies."

2. Categories of cookies we use

2.1 Strictly necessary cookies

These cookies are required for the Site to function and cannot be switched off. They are typically set only in response to actions you take, such as navigating between pages or submitting the quote-request form, and do not store personally identifying information. Because they are essential, they do not require consent under applicable law.

2.2 Functional cookies

These cookies allow the Site to remember choices you make (such as which quote-request tab — Commercial or Government — you last viewed) to provide a more personalized experience. If disabled, some parts of the Site may not function as smoothly.

2.3 Analytics & performance cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors use the Site — for example, which pages are viewed most often — so we can improve content and navigation. Where we use a third-party analytics provider (such as Google Analytics), that provider may set its own cookies subject to its own privacy policy. We do not currently use analytics cookies as of the effective date of this policy; if we add analytics tooling in the future, we will update this table and, where required by law, request your consent first.

2.4 Advertising / targeting cookies

Prime Clean does not currently use advertising or cross-site targeting cookies, and does not sell or share personal information collected through cookies for cross-context behavioral advertising. If this changes in the future, we will update this policy and provide an opt-out mechanism.

4. Third-party cookies

Where the Site embeds or links to third-party services — such as a phone-tracking number provider, a form-processing service, or an embedded map — those third parties may set their own cookies in accordance with their own privacy and cookie policies, which we encourage you to review. Prime Clean does not control third-party cookies and is not responsible for their content or practices.

5. California residents & tracking technologies

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), the use of certain tracking technologies to share data with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising can be considered a "sale" or "share" of personal information, even if no money changes hands. Prime Clean does not currently use cookies in this way. If that changes, we will (a) update this Cookie Policy and our Privacy Policy, and (b) honor opt-out preference signals such as the Global Privacy Control (GPC), where technically detectable by the Site, as a valid request to opt out of any future sale or sharing of personal information under California law.

6. Your choices

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View what cookies are stored and delete them individually
  • Block third-party cookies
  • Block cookies from particular sites
  • Block all cookies from being set
  • Delete all cookies when you close your browser

Because strictly necessary cookies are required for basic Site functionality, blocking all cookies may affect your ability to use certain features, such as the quote-request form's tab-switching behavior. Browser-specific instructions are typically available in each browser's help documentation (for example, under "Privacy and security" settings in Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge).

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time, including when we add new tools or services to the Site. We will post the updated policy on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
